Problem

Companies don't lose documents when people leave. They lose judgment. Policies record what was decided, never why. Regulated teams cannot paste consequential work into a public chatbot, so decisions that should take minutes wait days on meetings.

  • One-Press Huddle: live audio huddle with your management team, 24/7/365.
  • Wisdom Twin: role-specific judgment trained on 10-20 years of that role's decisions.
  • Trust Layer: cited evidence, permissions, safe decline, human override, audit. Private VPC / on-prem / air-gapped.
